# Juggernaut

> Hidden knowledge source. Retrieve selectively; never dump this file into the dashboard.

## Identity
Flexible lane/tempo carry: combine Blade Fury safety, Healing Ward sustain and Omnislash/Swiftslash isolation to pressure objectives and punish separated targets.

## Skills
- Blade Fury: kill/survival tool; avoid wasting it if magic immunity is needed for the next threat.
- Healing Ward: objective and reset resource; ward positioning often determines whether pressure continues.
- Omnislash: strongest on isolated high-value targets; track nearby units and defensive saves before committing.

## Items
- Build farming/attack-speed efficiency when the map allows scaling; accelerate fighting when your team can convert Omni windows.
- Manta/dispel, BKB, Butterfly/Skadi, Basher or Nullifier are matchup solutions rather than fixed sequence.
- Attack speed improves both farm and ultimate damage delivery; balance it against access and survivability.

## Neutral Items
- Attack speed/damage is strong when ultimate and right-click uptime are reliable.
- Defensive/status-resistance options help when you can be controlled outside Blade Fury.
- Mobility is valuable when it creates isolation for Omnislash or improves split-map pressure.

## Draft Fit
Prefer when Blade Fury answers key magic threats and enemy heroes are vulnerable to isolation. Be cautious into summons/illusion clutter, strong saves, physical mitigation and BKB-piercing lockdown.
