# Sven

> Hidden knowledge source. Retrieve selectively; never dump this file into the dashboard.

## Identity
Tempo physical carry: accelerate with cleave, then convert God’s Strength + mobility/BKB timings into decisive fights.

## Skills
- Great Cleave: farming accelerator; prioritize dense wave + stacked-camp routes.
- Warcry: use proactively for move speed/armor when entering or disengaging, not only after damage starts.
- God's Strength: major commitment window; avoid low-value fights when it is unavailable.

## Items
- Early farm/tempo: Power Treads + efficient sustain/farm components.
- Core decision: Blink for access; BKB when control prevents sustained hitting.
- Damage after access/survivability: choose crit, attack speed, dispel or anti-evasion according to targets.

## Neutral Items
- Prioritize attack speed/damage when you can connect reliably.
- Prefer survivability/status resistance when enemy control is the real limiter.
- Mobility is high value when it changes Blink follow-up or target access.

## Draft Fit
Prefer when your team can provide vision/setup and the enemy backline is punishable by burst initiation. Be cautious into heavy kiting, saves and repeated BKB-piercing control.
