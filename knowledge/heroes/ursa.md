# Ursa

> Hidden knowledge source. Retrieve selectively; never dump this file into the dashboard.

## Identity
Short-range tempo carry/Roshan threat: win through Fury Swipes stacking, repeated jump windows and forcing close fights.

## Skills
- Fury Swipes: long trades and Roshan scale rapidly; preserve contact on the same target.
- Overpower: synchronize with jump/disable windows rather than spending charges into kite.
- Enrage: defensive timing tool that lets you commit through burst/control windows.

## Items
- Early tempo: boots + lane/farm efficiency, then solve target access.
- Blink is often the key conversion item; BKB when spells stop contact.
- Basher/Abyssal, dispel or anti-kite options depend on whether lockdown or survivability limits kills.

## Neutral Items
- Attack speed has exceptional value when it improves Fury Swipes stacking.
- Movement/access effects are valuable against kite-heavy drafts.
- Durability/status resistance rises in value when you must survive the first control chain.

## Draft Fit
Prefer into melee/frontline heroes you can stay on and lineups that struggle to reset your engagement. Be cautious into layered slows, disengage, saves and elusive ranged cores.
