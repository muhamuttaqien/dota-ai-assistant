# Position 1 Carry Macro Knowledge

Hidden coaching knowledge for retrieval, not direct dashboard display.

- Optimize farm by risk-adjusted value, not raw camp count.
- Treat waves as information and map-pressure tools as well as gold.
- Join fights when timing, objective value, target access, and expected survival justify opportunity cost.
- Convert enemy rotations into opposite-side waves, camps, towers, Roshan, or vision-safe territory.
- Itemize to solve the current limiting factor: access, survival, damage, dispel, anti-heal, anti-evasion, or lockdown.
- Protect buyback and high-ground conditions when one death can decide the game.
